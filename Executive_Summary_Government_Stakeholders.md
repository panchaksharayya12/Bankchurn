## 1. Executive Mandate & Purpose
This executive summary provides a high-level supervisory evaluation of retail depositor stability, account portability, and capital concentration risks across 10,000 retail banking accounts in Germany, France, and Spain.

Under the framework of European financial integration, consumer mobility initiatives (such as SEPA account-switching directives and PSD2/Open Banking regulations) have accelerated depositor churn. While increased competition fosters consumer welfare, unchecked asymmetric customer flight can precipitate localized liquidity strain and weaken the deposit funding base of credit institutions.

---

## 2. Core Macro Findings & Prudential Risk Indicators

### 2.1 The German Market Risk Anomaly
* **Prudential Observation:** German retail banking accounts exhibit a **32.44%** annual churn rate, compared to **16.15%** in France and **16.67%** in Spain.
* **Geographic Risk Index (GRI):** Germany registers a systemic risk multiplier of **1.59×** against the European baseline.
* **Systemic Implication:** Although Germany accounts for only 25% of the evaluated customer cohort, it represents **39.96%** of all churned accounts. This rapid turnover signals heightened domestic competition, fee sensitivity, and aggressive deposit cannibalization by digital neobanks operating under EU passporting licenses.

### 2.2 Pre-Retirement Capital Flight (Ages 46–60)
* **Demographic Vulnerability:** Across all three jurisdictions, clients aged **46–60** present the highest churn rate at **51.12%**. In Germany, this figure escalates to **67.33%** (more than 2 out of 3 customers).
* **Prudential Implication:** This demographic cohort commands the highest average personal wealth, pension accumulation, and deposit balances. High churn in this bracket represents an accelerating drain of prime retail deposits into non-bank alternative investment funds or foreign digital platforms.

### 2.3 Retail Deposit Concentration & Liquidity Exposure
* **Aggregate Balance Drain:** Churned accounts across the 10,000-customer sample represent **€185.59 Million** in departed capital.
* **High-Balance Exposure:** Churned high-balance accounts ($\ge €119,839$) alone account for **€110.85 Million** (60% of total balance churn).
* **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Impact:** Rapid outflows of operational retail deposits can adversely impact a bank's stable funding ratio and necessitate reliance on costlier wholesale liquidity.

### 2.4 Multi-Product Attrition Paradox
* **Finding:** While clients with 2 products demonstrate high loyalty (**7.58%** churn), clients holding 3 products experience **82.71%** churn, and 4 products experience **100%** churn.
* **Supervisory Concern:** This severe cliff suggests predatory cross-selling, complex product bundling, or teaser rate expirations that lead to widespread customer dissatisfaction, prompting regulatory scrutiny under EU Consumer Fairness and TCF (Treating Customers Fairly) standards.

## 4. Policy Recommendations for Regulators & Banking Executives

### For Credit Institutions (Tier-1 / Tier-2 Banks):
1. **Dynamic Liquidity Stress Testing:** Incorporate granular age- and country-stratified depositor flight rates into Pillar 2 Internal Capital and Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Processes (ICAAP / ILAAP).
2. **Review of Multi-Product Fee Structures:** Eliminate punitive terms on secondary and tertiary bundled accounts that trigger retaliatory account cancellations.
3. **Targeted Advisory for Retiring Demographics:** Establish specialized financial wellness and wealth transition desks for depositors entering the 46–60 age bracket.

### For Supervisory & Government Bodies:
1. **Monitoring Deposit Migration Trends:** Track cross-border deposit flight from traditional brick-and-mortar lenders into digital institutions to ensure deposit insurance schemes remain resilient.
2. **Standardization of Churn Reporting:** Mandate standardized disclosures of segment-level churn rates in regulatory reporting to detect operational weaknesses prior to capital impairment.
3. **Consumer Protection in Promotional Bundling:** Issue guidelines prohibiting aggressive teaser-rate expirations that drive retail consumers into cyclical account switching.
